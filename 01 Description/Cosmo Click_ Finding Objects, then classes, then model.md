# `Problem:` 

```
The cosmetology venture “MaGaby Home Spa” currently offers
various beauty services and sells personal skincare products.
The company handles its advertising and customer service through
various social media platforms (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp).
In addition, the company chooses to manage its products,
services, and inventory manually.
```

```
This management method makes administrative tasks quite time-
consuming for the company and increases the risk of information
loss or becoming outdated, which could negatively impact the
venture in the future.
```

```
For this reason, the goal is to develop software—specifically an
app—to improve the management and organization of “MaGaby Home
Spa” in areas such as customers, appointments, services,
products, sales, and inventory, thereby streamlining the
handling of this information.
```

## `Overview:` 

```
In the field of cosmetology, aesthetics, and body wellness,
clients seek to schedule specialized treatments (facial, body,
and hair) and receive personalized follow-up based on their
health history and skin type. Currently, manual customer service
and management creates scheduling difficulties, confusion
regarding service rates, and financial losses due to the
expiration of low-turnover products. Cosmo Click seeks to
digitize and automate this experience for both
cosmetologists/employees and clients.
```

## `Background:` 

```
The business offers facial, body, and scalp aesthetic
treatments, along with related products. Its services include
facial cleansing and hydration, anti-aging and anti-acne
treatments, wood therapy, lymphatic drainage, massages, scalp
treatments, and other aesthetic procedures. Clients typically
consist of family, friends, acquaintances, and referrals;
currently, customer service and appointment scheduling are
handled via WhatsApp.
```

```
Current operations face several challenges: organizing
appointments, avoiding pricing confusion during booking, and
managing the inventory of products and materials. Some products
```

```
have low demand and risk expiration, whereas high-demand
services require larger stocks of materials and tools.
```

```
The proposal involves developing an application for both clients
and staff that consolidates service information and supports
day-to-day management. The app will allow users to view
available services, request and schedule appointments, maintain
client diagnostic records, send reminders, and generate reports
on the most popular products and services. It will also
facilitate payment via bank transfer. The goal is to streamline
business organization and optimize the time spent on these
tasks.
```
